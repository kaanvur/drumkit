🥁 Finally built my dream desk toy - a fully 3D printed Bluetooth MIDI drum kit!

Been working on this for a while and super happy with how it turned out. It's a compact electronic drum kit that connects wirelessly to your Mac, phone, or tablet via Bluetooth MIDI.

**What makes it cool:**
- 5 pads (kick, snare, hi-hat, tom, cymbal) - all 3D printed
- ESP32-C3 Super Mini as the brain (tiny but powerful!)
- Piezo sensors for velocity sensitivity - hit soft for quiet notes, hit hard for loud ones
- Works with GarageBand, BandLab, n-track Studio, and more
- No wires to your computer - just Bluetooth!

**Parts list is minimal:**
- ESP32-C3 Super Mini (~$3)
- 5 piezo sensors (~$5)
- 5 resistors (~$1)
- Some jumper wires
- PLA filament for printing

Total cost under $15 (excluding the 3D printer obviously 😄)

The .3mf file has everything ready to print - 6 build plates with color coding. Just slice and print!
